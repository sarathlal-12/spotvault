Features

Everything your offline library needs

Paste a playlist link once, or search for a single song. SpotVault keeps each folder up to date and fills in the details music players care about.

Any playlist link NEW

Spotify, YouTube, YouTube Music and SoundCloud playlists all work. Each one becomes its own folder of songs.

Search any song NEW

Missing a track? Search for it, listen first, then save it straight into any playlist, from your PC or your phone.

Cover art & tags

Clean studio songs, never movie dialogue or video intros. Album art, title, artist and album are written into every file.

Smart re-sync

Added songs to a playlist? Press Sync and only the new ones download. Songs already on disk are skipped.

Wi-Fi phone sync

Send every playlist to your Android phone or iPhone with one tap. Folders, order and artwork arrive exactly as on your PC.

Private by design

No sign-in and no analytics in the apps. Your music moves straight from your PC to your phone and never touches a server.

How it works

From playlist link to your pocket

Add a playlist

Copy a playlist link from Spotify, YouTube or SoundCloud, click + Add playlist and give it a folder name.

Sync on your PC

Click Sync. Songs download with art and tags; the activity panel shows each one as it lands.

Send to your phone

Click Connect phone, open the phone app on the same Wi-Fi and tap Sync now.

New in v1.4

Your whole library, on your phone

SpotVault Mobile copies every playlist from your PC over Wi-Fi. Now you can also search for a song on the phone, listen to it, and save it to a playlist. Your PC does the downloading and sends it straight back.

  • Add songs from the phone. Search, tap to listen, tap Save, pick a playlist. It arrives with cover art, and Ghost Queue plays it next as soon as it lands.
  • A fresh look. Glowing, animated player in dark or light mode, switched with one tap.
  • Moments and A–B Loop. Pin your favourite part of a song and jump back to it, or repeat one part to learn it.
  • Smart Sleep and Rewind. The sleep timer fades out gently, and Rewind shows how much you listened this month.
  • Finds your PC automatically. Or type the address shown on the PC.
  • PIN protected. Only phones with your 6-digit PIN can connect.
  • Only new songs. Later syncs copy what changed, and a stopped sync picks up where it left off.
  • Stays in step. Songs you delete on the PC can be removed from the phone too.

Not on the App Store. How to install it

Latest: v1.4.0 · All versions & release notes

iPhone

Install on iPhone with AltStore

SpotVault isn't on the App Store, so you install it yourself with your own Apple ID. It takes about 10 minutes the first time.

Step by step (Windows PC)

You need your iPhone, its cable and your Apple ID.

  1. Install iTunes and iCloud using the direct (non-Microsoft Store) installers linked in the AltStore install guide. Uninstall the Store versions first if you have them.
  2. Install AltServer from altstore.io and run it. It lives in the system tray.
  3. Plug in your iPhone, tap Trust, then click the AltServer tray icon → Install AltStore → your iPhone, and sign in with your Apple ID.
  4. On the iPhone, open Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, tap your Apple ID and tap Trust. On iOS 16+, also turn on Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode.
  5. Download the IPA on the iPhone in Safari (button above). In the Files app, tap it → Share → AltStore. AltStore installs SpotVault.
  6. Open SpotVault → Sync tab. Type the address from Connect phone on your PC and the PIN, then allow Local Network access.
Get updates in AltStore: on your iPhone, tap Add SpotVault to AltStore. AltStore then shows Update for SpotVault whenever a new version is out. If the link does nothing, open AltStore → Sources → + and paste https://spotvault-web.vercel.app/altstore.json.
Keep it working: with a free Apple ID, apps expire after 7 days. Leave AltServer running on your PC and turn on Wi-Fi sync for your iPhone in iTunes once; AltStore then renews SpotVault automatically when both are on the same Wi-Fi.
Type the address: the Scan button can't find your PC on iPhone (Apple blocks Wi-Fi discovery for sideloaded apps). Enter the address shown in the Connect phone window instead.
Prefer one-off installs? Sideloadly also works: install iTunes, plug in the phone, drag the IPA in and sign in. Reinstall every 7 days.
Bonus: synced songs also show up in the iPhone Files app under SpotVault.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Does my playlist need to be public?
Yes. Use a public playlist or one shared by link. Private playlists can't be read.
The phone can't find my PC. What do I check?
Put both on the same Wi-Fi (not a guest network) and keep SpotVault open on the PC. The first time, Windows Firewall asks about network access: allow SpotVault on Private networks. You can also type the address shown in the Connect phone window.
Will songs have movie dialogue or video intros?
No. SpotVault looks for the clean studio song first on YouTube Music, then SoundCloud, and only then on YouTube, where it picks lyric videos and skips video songs, scenes, trailers, loops and remixes. Even a YouTube playlist full of movie video songs is saved as the clean songs.
Can I add songs from my phone?
Yes. Open Search → Find new songs, type a song, tap it to listen, then tap download and pick a playlist. Your PC downloads it and sends it back, so keep SpotVault open on the PC and both devices on the same Wi-Fi.
How do I update from an older version?
From v1.3 on, SpotVault tells you when an update is ready and installs it for you. Coming from v1.1: on Windows and iPhone, install v1.3 over the old app and everything is kept. On Android, uninstall v1.1 first, install v1.3, then sync from your PC again (this one-time step is needed because of a change in how the app is signed).
How do I install the Android app?
Download SpotVault_v1.4.0.apk on your phone, open it, and allow installing apps from your browser or file manager when Android asks.
Why isn't SpotVault on the App Store?
It's a free side project, so it's shared as an IPA you install yourself. Follow the iPhone guide: AltStore signs the app with your own Apple ID and keeps it renewed.
Why does the first sync take a while?
Each song is found, downloaded, converted and tagged. Later syncs only fetch new songs, so they're much faster. Everything the app needs is built in, so there is nothing extra to install.
Where are my songs saved?
On the PC, in your Music folder by default (change it under Music folder). Each playlist gets its own folder. On the phone, inside the SpotVault app's storage (on iPhone you can also see them in the Files app).
