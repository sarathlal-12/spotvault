1.0 / Clean Audio & Lyric-Only Filter

+
Music videos often contain minute-long intro dialogue, car crash sound effects, movie cuts, or quiet outro sequences. SpotVault’s filtering heuristics target official audio tracks and verified lyric uploads, stripping out non-musical audio interruptions.

2.0 / Persistent State & Auto-Resume

+
Never download the same song twice. SpotVault verifies track hashes and existing ID3 metadata in your local destination folder. If an acquisition session is stopped or internet connection drops, it automatically resumes starting right from the last interrupted song.

3.0 / Multi-Playlist Batch Acquisition

+
Queue multiple Spotify playlist URLs simultaneously. SpotVault maintains separate destination directory allocations for each playlist, isolates threads, and tags folders without mix-up.
TRI-PROVIDER PIPELINE

YouTube Music, SoundCloud & YouTube

If a track is region-restricted on one service or missing from YouTube Music, the engine immediately shifts fallback evaluation to SoundCloud and YouTube query indices to secure the highest-bitrate stream.

METADATA RETENTION

Embedded Covers & ID3v2.4 Tags

Dynamic track numbers, artist rosters, year stamps, and original high-resolution album covers are permanently baked into the audio binary headers for seamless car and offline player compatibility.

FAULT RESILIENCE

Directory Orphan Recovery

Re-organized your library or renamed a folder? SpotVault checks target folders before initiating network requests, preventing duplicate downloads or orphaned broken files.

PRIVACY GUARANTEE

100% Local & Zero Telemetry

No account logins required, no analytics pinged, and zero usage telemetry sent to third-party databases. Direct socket execution runs completely isolated on your machine.
