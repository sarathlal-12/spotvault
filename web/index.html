Take the tour

One app, every way you listen.

01 · SAVE

Any song, saved for good.

Search any song on your phone, listen first, then save it into a playlist. It downloads right on the phone with cover art, and the clean studio version is picked, never movie dialogue or video intros.

  • Add songs from the web inside any playlist
  • Big Spotify or YouTube playlist? The Windows app saves the whole thing
02 · PLAY

A calm player that feels premium.

The cover breathes in a soft wavy frame while a thin ring fills as the song plays. Queue, A-B loop, sleep timer and sound tools sit one tap away.

  • "Play next" really plays next
  • Minimal or the classic Crimson look, light or dark
03 · SING

Live lyrics that follow along.

Swipe up and the line being sung lights up and stays in view. Tap any line to jump there. Lyrics are saved, so they work offline after the first time.

04 · JAM

Jam with friends.

One phone plays on the speaker while everyone adds songs, or every phone plays the same moment, as one big speaker. Friends join by scanning a QR code.

  • Shared timeline and queue
  • Speaker delay fix for Bluetooth speakers
  • Saved phones join with one tap
05 · DRIVE

Made for the car, too.

Car mode gives you huge buttons and swipe-to-skip. On Android Auto, SpotVault shows your playlists right on the car screen, with voice search.

iPhone

Install on iPhone with AltStore.

SpotVault isn't on the App Store, so you install it with your own Apple ID. About 10 minutes the first time.

  1. Install iTunes and iCloud using the direct (non-Microsoft Store) installers linked in the AltStore install guide.
  2. Install AltServer from altstore.io and run it. It lives in the system tray.
  3. Plug in your iPhone, tap Trust, then click the AltServer tray icon → Install AltStore → your iPhone, and sign in with your Apple ID.
  4. On the iPhone: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → trust your Apple ID. On iOS 16+, turn on Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode.
  5. Download the IPA in Safari. In Files, tap it → Share → AltStore. AltStore installs SpotVault.
  6. Open SpotVault, search a song and save it. Using the Windows app too? Sync → Scan the code on your PC, then allow Local Network access.
Get updates in AltStore: on your iPhone, add SpotVault to AltStore. If the link does nothing, open AltStore → Sources → + and paste https://spotvault-web.vercel.app/altstore.json
Keep it working: with a free Apple ID, apps expire after 7 days. Leave AltServer running and turn on Wi-Fi sync for your iPhone in iTunes once; AltStore renews SpotVault automatically on the same Wi-Fi.
Prefer one-off installs? Sideloadly works too. Reinstall every 7 days.
Bonus: synced songs also show up in the iPhone Files app under SpotVault.

Questions, answered.

Do I need a PC?

No. The phone app finds, downloads and plays songs on its own. The Windows app is an extra for saving whole big playlists at once and listening on your PC.

Does my playlist need to be public?

Yes. Use a public playlist or one shared by link. Private playlists can't be read.

The phone can't find my PC. What do I check?

Put both on the same Wi-Fi (not a guest network) and keep SpotVault open on the PC. The first time, allow SpotVault on Private networks if Windows Firewall asks. Easiest: on the PC open Settings → Phone link and scan the code from the phone's Sync tab.

How does Jam work? Do songs get copied?

In "Be the speaker", songs your friends add are sent to the speaker phone and kept only until the Jam ends. In "Everyone in sync", each phone plays the same moment, as one big speaker. Friends join by scanning the Jam's QR code or tapping a saved phone. To keep a friend's song, use Share instead.

Can friends join a Jam from somewhere else?

Yes. On the Jam screen, tap Start under "Jam over the internet" and send the 6-letter code, QR or link. You let each friend in, and everyone hears the same song at the same moment. Friends can pause, skip and add songs. Each phone plays its own copy, or streams the song if it doesn't have it.

Can I share songs without Wi-Fi?

Yes, over Bluetooth. The other phone opens Jam and turns on Receive by Bluetooth, then you pick Share → Bluetooth. Android to Android and iPhone to iPhone are fast. iPhone to Android works too, but slowly (about 1 to 3 minutes a song), so Wi-Fi or a hotspot is better for those.

Will songs have movie dialogue or video intros?

No. SpotVault looks for the clean studio song first on YouTube Music, then SoundCloud, and only then on YouTube, where it picks lyric videos and skips scenes, trailers, loops and remixes.

Can I add songs from my phone?

Yes. Search → Find new songs, tap a song to listen, then Save it into a playlist. Or open a playlist and tap Add songs from the web. The phone downloads it by itself.

How do I update?

SpotVault tells you when an update is ready: press Check for updates on Windows (Settings → Updates) or on the phone (Sync → More). On iPhone, AltStore shows the update. Updating keeps your playlists, songs and settings.

Does it work in the car?

Yes. Car mode in Studio gives you big buttons. On Android Auto, SpotVault shows your playlists on the car screen. Over Bluetooth and CarPlay, the song and controls show like any music app.

Where are my songs saved?

On the PC, in your Music folder by default, one folder per playlist. On the phone, inside the SpotVault app's storage (on iPhone you can also see them in the Files app).
